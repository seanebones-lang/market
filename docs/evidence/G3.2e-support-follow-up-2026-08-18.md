## Purpose

This append-only record preserves the sanitized outcome of two follow-up contacts after the
original support escalation. It does not amend
`docs/research/G3.2e-BEST-PRICE-COHERENCE-RESOLUTION.md`, reinterpret an endpoint response,
authorize network collection, or change the blocked gate status.

The follow-ups were made because the original human frontline agent did not answer the endpoint
semantics questions, and no email acknowledgment of the stated back-end escalation was received.

## First follow-up: technical escalation status

A new authenticated support conversation asked Robinhood to link the contact to existing case
`021436592` and route the five unresolved `V2BestBidAsk` questions to Crypto API engineering or
developer support. The message restated only the already-sanitized facts:

- successful authenticated read-only access to the documented best-price endpoint;
- three validation cycles, including two `ask_lt_bid` relation outcomes;
- presence of the undocumented `timestamp` field;
- rejection and non-retention of the crossed values; and
- willingness to provide only sanitized timing, HTTP class, row-count, timestamp-presence, and
  relation-category metadata if engineering requires it.

A second human Robinhood Crypto agent checked the case and did not provide or claim a technical
semantics answer. The agent confirmed that another colleague is handling the concern, the case is
already escalated to Robinhood's back-end team, future correspondence will remain under case
`021436592`, and Robinhood will contact the account owner when the back-end team responds.

## Second follow-up: missing email acknowledgment

Because no email acknowledgment had arrived, another authenticated support contact asked Robinhood
to confirm that the case is active, state whether an acknowledgment had been generated, resend it
to the email address already registered on the account, provide the expected sender and subject,
and avoid opening a duplicate case. No email address was typed or disclosed in the conversation.

The same human agent explicitly recognized case `021436592`, checked the account, and confirmed
that the concern will continue under that case and that future review updates will be sent by
email. The agent did not answer whether an acknowledgment email had ever been generated, did not
resend an acknowledgment, and did not provide an expected sender address or subject line.

The evidentiary distinction is therefore:

- **Case existence and continuity:** confirmed by two human support follow-ups.
- **Existing back-end escalation:** confirmed by two human support follow-ups.
- **Future update channel:** stated to be email through the existing case.
- **Acknowledgment email generation or delivery:** not confirmed.
- **Endpoint field or crossed-row semantics:** still unanswered by a human or back-end source.

- `bid` remains the sell touch and `ask` remains the buy touch.
- `ask >= bid` remains mandatory for an admissible best-price observation.
- The undocumented `timestamp` remains compatibility-only and excluded from persistence and
  derivation.
- No retry, reinterpretation, field swap, clipping, quote selection, or later-response
  substitution is authorized.
- No ordinary preflight, fallback diagnostic, production schedule, 30-day collection, strategy
  evaluation, order, transfer, or capital use is authorized.
- The planning deadline in ADR 0004 remains `2026-08-31T17:00:00-05:00`.

Case confirmation establishes that the vendor dependency is active; it does not satisfy the
resolution-success criteria. G3.2e remains blocked pending a technically useful back-end response
or the separately governed no-useful-response branch.
