## Implemented contract

- `next_bar_open` remains the exact, cost-free timing baseline and rejects nonzero spread or
  slippage inputs.
- `next_bar_open_bid_ask` treats next open as a synthetic reference mid, crosses the side-specific
  synthetic touch, and applies slippage adversely from that touch.
- The full quoted-spread and adverse-slippage inputs are exact, finite, nonnegative `Decimal`
  assumptions with bounds that keep derived prices positive.
- Input and artifact names include `assumption`; no generated touch is labeled observed market or
  venue data.
- Every run summary records the selected model and both assumptions.
- Eligible-order, fill, and rejected-execution events record the reference open, synthetic bid and
  ask, pre-slippage touch, simulated fill price, and assumptions.
- Fill artifacts preserve the same provenance. Artifact schema version is 3.
