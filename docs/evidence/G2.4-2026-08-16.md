## Implemented contract

Remaining BTC is no longer converted to cash by an invisible end-of-run adjustment. After the
final bar closes and any ineligible end-of-data strategy order expires, the engine emits:

```text
terminal_liquidation_requested -> sell fill -> post_terminal_liquidation equity point
```

The fill sells the full remaining inventory, applies the run's execution and venue-cost contracts,
and leaves `final_position_btc=0`. No terminal request or fill is created when the portfolio is
already flat.

## Terminal execution price

The final candle close is explicitly treated as a synthetic reference mid, not as an observed
executable quote.

- `next_bar_open` maps to `last_bar_close` and uses the final close as the terminal sell price.
- `next_bar_open_bid_ask` maps to `last_bar_close_bid_ask`, derives the synthetic bid and ask from
  the run's full-spread assumption, and applies adverse sell slippage from the bid.

For the golden `$20` final close, 20-bps full spread, and 10-bps adverse slippage:

```text
synthetic bid = 20 * (1 - 20 / (2 * 10,000)) = 19.980
sell fill     = 19.980 * (1 - 10 / 10,000)   = 19.960020
```

The terminal fill and event record `reference_close_usd`, both synthetic touches, the
pre-slippage touch, final price, named assumptions, venue profile, and liquidation reason.

## Cost proof

The Robinhood v2 golden replay uses a 95-bps per-fill assumption:

| Fill | Quantity | Price | Executed notional | Fee |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strategy buy | `1 BTC` | `$20.040020` | `$20.040020` | `$0.1903801900` |
| Terminal sell | `1 BTC` | `$19.960020` | `$19.960020` | `$0.1896201900` |
| Total | | | `$40.000040` | `$0.3800003800` |

The replay ends at `$999.5399996200`, a loss of `$0.4600003800`, and zero BTC. Robinhood v1
coverage separately proves a `$19.8080` terminal sell under a 192-bps full-spread assumption with
zero separate transaction fee. The legacy baseline proves both the entry and terminal fill pay its
declared per-fill fee.

## Artifact contract

Schema version 6 adds:

```text
terminal_liquidation_model
position_before_terminal_liquidation_btc
terminal_liquidation_fills
terminal_liquidation_qty_btc
terminal_liquidation_fee_usd
```

Equity rows now carry a `stage`. A liquidated run ends with a
`post_terminal_liquidation` point containing final cash, zero BTC, and final net equity. This makes
the difference between the final bar-close mark and the fully costed liquidation result visible.

## Validation coverage

- Terminal sell uses the final close rather than a nonexistent next bar.
- Spread moves the sell to the synthetic bid and adverse slippage moves it farther downward.
- v1 spread-inclusive and v2 exchange-taker cost treatments remain distinct.
- The transaction fee is calculated on terminal executed notional and included in cumulative fees.
- `terminal_liquidation_requested` precedes the terminal fill with a shared deterministic client
  order ID.
- Pre-liquidation inventory is reported separately from the flat final position.
- Already-flat and last-bar-order-expiration paths create no terminal fill.
- Summary, events, fills, and staged equity artifacts expose the new contract.

## Remaining blockers

G2.5 through G2.10 remain open. The current engine does not yet have double-entry-style portfolio
accounting, complete trade lifecycle metrics, benchmarks, research statistics, or reproducible run
identity. G3 walk-forward research and all live-money stages remain locked.
