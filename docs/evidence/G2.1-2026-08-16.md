## Implemented contract

- A close-confirmed bar is marked before strategy evaluation.
- A decision is timestamped after that bar closes.
- An accepted order becomes eligible at the next contiguous bar open.
- The sole current execution model is explicitly named `next_bar_open`.
- Fills record signal, decision, eligibility, and fill provenance.
- Ordered `bar_open`, `bar_close`, decision, eligibility, fill, rejection, and expiration events are
  retained in memory and written to `events.jsonl`.
- A final-bar decision expires without a fill.
