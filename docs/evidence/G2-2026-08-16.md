## Known limits carried into G3

- Robinhood costs are configured assumptions, not account-observed values.
- The OHLC engine does not invent partial executions; broker-like partial streams are tested in the
  lifecycle/accounting analyzers.
- Annualized statistics on short samples are descriptive and can be extreme.
- No walk-forward, bootstrap, multiple-testing, parameter-stability, or untouched-holdout result
  exists yet.
- No evidence currently demonstrates that EMA crossover is profitable.
