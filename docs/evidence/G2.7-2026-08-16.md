## Implemented contract

Every nonempty backtest now produces three deterministic comparators:

| Benchmark | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Cash | Holds starting cash with zero executions, costs, return, and drawdown |
| Matched-notional buy-and-hold | Executes one opening buy and one fully costed terminal sell |
| Periodic DCA | Splits the same gross buy notional across fixed bar intervals, then sells at the end |

The matched gross notional is the maximum concurrent gross inventory cost basis in the strategy's
immutable accounting journal. It is not cumulative buy turnover, which could count reused capital
multiple times. The allocation is capped only when the requested gross notional plus its configured
entry fee would exceed starting cash:

```text
applied matched notional
  = min(strategy peak gross inventory cost basis,
        starting cash / (1 + configured per-fill fee rate))
```

Both invested benchmarks retain unused cash. Their buys use the run's side-specific synthetic
bar-open execution price. Their final sells use the last close's synthetic bid, adverse slippage,
and per-fill fee. Periodic DCA starts at bar zero, defaults to every 168 bars, and exposes a positive
integer `benchmark_dca_interval_bars` configuration.

## Absolute and risk-adjusted comparison

Absolute comparison reports strategy-minus-benchmark after-fee P&L and return percentage points.
G2.7 uses one deliberately limited risk-adjusted measure:

```text
net P&L over maximum drawdown
  = final net P&L after fees / maximum dollar net-liquidation-value drawdown
```

It is undefined when maximum drawdown is zero. Cash comparisons therefore carry an explicit
`benchmark_zero_drawdown` or `both_zero_drawdown` status rather than a fabricated zero or infinity.
This measure is not annualized and is not labeled Sharpe or Calmar. The broader research-statistics
contract remains G2.8.

## Hand-calculated three-bar proof

The golden fixture starts with `$1,000`, matches `$200` of peak strategy gross basis, applies a 1%
fee on every fill, and uses executable prices of `$100`, `$80`, and `$200`.

### Matched-notional buy-and-hold

```text
buy:  2 BTC @ $100 = $200 notional + $2 fee
sell: 2 BTC @ $200 = $400 notional - $4 fee
final cash / net P&L = $1,194 / +$194
bar-close NLV curve  = $996, $956.40, $1,194
maximum drawdown     = $43.60
P&L / max drawdown   = 194 / 43.60
```

### Two-installment periodic DCA

```text
bar 0 buy: 1 BTC @ $100 = $100 notional + $1 fee
bar 2 buy: 0.5 BTC @ $200 = $100 notional + $1 fee
final sell: 1.5 BTC @ $200 = $300 notional - $3 fee
final cash / net P&L = $1,095 / +$95
bar-close NLV curve  = $998, $978.20, $1,095
maximum drawdown     = $21.80
P&L / max drawdown   = 95 / 21.80
```

Both invested benchmarks execute exactly `$200` of gross buys. The fixture therefore proves that
the difference comes from schedule and resulting inventory, not unequal deployed notional.

## Artifact contract

Schema version 9 adds:

- `benchmarks.jsonl`: one benchmark contract, three results, and three comparisons;
- `benchmark_fills.jsonl`: every passive entry and terminal fill with benchmark identity,
  execution model, and venue-cost profile; and
- `benchmark_equity.jsonl`: every benchmark's costed net-liquidation value at every bar close.

The main summary also embeds the contract, results, and comparisons. CLI output shows applied
matched notional, DCA interval, absolute results, maximum drawdown, risk-adjusted ratios, and
explicit undefined states.

## Failure-path coverage

- Negative/non-finite starting cash, non-finite strategy P&L, and negative/non-finite strategy
  drawdown fail closed.
- Zero, negative, Boolean, and noninteger DCA intervals fail closed.
- Missing, mismatched, noncontiguous, or nonreconciling strategy journals fail closed.
- Negative/non-finite strategy cost basis fails closed.
- Missing, unordered, nonpositive, or non-finite benchmark market points fail closed.
- Positive matched notional without executable market points fails closed.
- Passive terminal liquidation must equal the preceding costed net-liquidation estimate exactly.
- Zero strategy exposure deterministically collapses both matched comparators to cash.
