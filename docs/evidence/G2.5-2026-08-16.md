## Implemented contract

The backtester no longer maintains parallel ad hoc cash, BTC, and fee variables. One exact-Decimal
`PortfolioAccount` owns:

```text
cash
BTC inventory
weighted-average inventory cost basis
realized gross P&L
cumulative fees
immutable opening/fill journal entries
```

Every ordinary and terminal fill posts through the same account. Each frozen journal entry records
the cash, inventory, cost-basis, realized-gross-P&L, and fee deltas plus all resulting balances. It
links to the corresponding backtest fill event by `event_sequence`; the fill links back by
`accounting_journal_sequence`. Event sequences must increase monotonically.

## Accounting convention

Cost basis is gross executed buy notional and excludes fees. Realized and unrealized P&L are also
gross, while fees remain a separate cumulative account:

```text
buy basis increase    = buy quantity * buy fill price
allocated sell basis  = pre-sell basis * sold quantity / pre-sell inventory
realized gross P&L    = sell notional - allocated basis
unrealized gross P&L  = inventory * mark price - remaining basis

marked equity
  = cash + inventory * mark price
  = starting cash + realized gross P&L + unrealized gross P&L - cumulative fees
```

Every fill journal entry and mark records an `accounting_identity_residual_usd`. The engine raises
if it is nonzero.

## Marked equity versus net liquidation value

Marked equity values BTC at the candle-close reference mid. Net liquidation value estimates actual
cash after selling all open BTC with the run's declared spread, adverse slippage, and transaction
fee assumptions:

```text
net liquidation value
  = cash + inventory * estimated liquidation sell price - estimated liquidation fee
```

These values deliberately differ while inventory is open. For a flat portfolio they converge to
cash. Net-liquidation drawdown and return now use the costed liquidation view instead of silently
mixing it with the marked-equity view.

## Hand-calculated partial-sale proof

Starting with `$1,000`, buy `2 BTC @ $100` with a `$1` fee:

| Account | Result |
|---|---:|
| Cash | `$799` |
| BTC inventory | `2` |
| Gross cost basis | `$200` |
| Realized gross P&L | `$0` |
| Cumulative fees | `$1` |

At a `$110` mark, marked equity is `$1,019`. A `$109` liquidation price and `$2` estimated exit
fee produce a separate `$1,015` net liquidation value. Selling `1 BTC @ $120` with a `$1.20` fee
allocates `$100` of basis, realizes `$20` gross P&L, leaves `1 BTC` and `$100` basis, increases cash
to `$917.80`, and increases cumulative fees to `$2.20`. At a `$115` mark, the identity reconciles:

```text
$1,032.80 marked equity
  = $1,000 starting cash + $20 realized gross + $15 unrealized gross - $2.20 fees
```

## Failure-path coverage

- Insufficient-cash buys, inventory-exceeding sells, zero quantities, zero prices, and negative fees
  fail before mutation.
- Negative and non-finite opening balances fail closed.
- Nonpositive/non-finite marks, nonpositive liquidation prices, negative estimated exit fees, and
  a nonzero exit fee on flat inventory fail closed.
- Duplicate or decreasing fill-event sequences fail before mutation.
- A full sale canonicalizes inventory and basis to exact zero.
- Journal entries are frozen and callers receive a tuple rather than the mutable internal list.
