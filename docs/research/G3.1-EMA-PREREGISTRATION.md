# G3.1 Preregistration: Long-Only BTC-USD EMA Crossover

- **Protocol version:** 1.0
- **Preregistered:** 2026-08-17
- **Strategy:** `slow_trend_v1`
- **Research gate:** G3.1
- **Status:** Binding before G3.2 split construction and before any G3 strategy run
- **Live-money authorization:** None

## Purpose and binding effect

This protocol defines the hypothesis, economic rationale, admissible information, complete
parameter family, primary endpoint, risk budget, and rejection rules for the first G3 study. Its
purpose is to make failure visible and to prevent results from changing the question after the
fact.

No result from the five-year G1 dataset may be used to change this protocol. G3.2 must freeze the
walk-forward windows and the final untouched holdout before any parameter is evaluated. A material
change after that point creates a new hypothesis version, requires a new preregistration and a new
untouched holdout, and cannot inherit confirmatory status from this study.

This document does not claim that the strategy is profitable. It authorizes research design only.

## Prior knowledge and contamination statement

The strategy and its default `12/26` EMA pair existed before this preregistration. Invalid legacy
backtests over 600 and 1,500 bars are also known. Those runs used look-ahead timing, incomplete
costs, incorrect terminal treatment, and unrecoverable inputs; they are not evidence, but their
existence means the strategy family is not a discovery made on a pristine dataset.

The G1 dataset has already been inspected for data quality and broad quarterly regime coverage.
Its quality report contains period returns and volatility labels. No G3 parameter comparison,
walk-forward result, or whole-dataset strategy result has been produced before protocol version
1.0. The final holdout frozen in G3.2 is therefore the only confirmatory sample. Development and
walk-forward results remain model-selection evidence and must be labeled as such.

## Hypothesis

### Alternative hypothesis (H1)

On hourly BTC-USD spot data, a long-only dual-EMA crossover can capture persistent intraday-to-
multi-day price trends while avoiding some sustained declining periods. After next-bar execution,
conservative Robinhood v2 cost assumptions, and all terminal costs, a stable EMA neighborhood will
have positive out-of-sample closed-trade expectancy and a better net-P&L-to-maximum-drawdown ratio
than matched-notional buy-and-hold.

### Null hypothesis (H0)

After realistic execution timing, costs, multiplicity, and parameter instability are considered,
the strategy's out-of-sample expectancy is not economically positive. Apparent performance is
explainable by noise, a small number of trades or periods, a narrow parameter peak, benchmark
exposure differences, or cost underestimation.

### Primary estimand and hurdle

For each closed trade `i`:

```text
net_trade_return_bps_i
  = 10,000 * realized_net_pnl_after_fees_i / gross_entry_notional_i
```

The primary estimand is the arithmetic mean of `net_trade_return_bps_i` across the stitched
walk-forward out-of-sample trades. The economically meaningful hurdle is **+10 bps per closed
trade after primary costs**. The one-sided 95% dependence-aware block-bootstrap lower confidence
bound must exceed +10 bps. Merely obtaining a positive point estimate does not pass.

G3.5 must freeze the exact block-bootstrap implementation before computing this interval. It may
choose a defensible block construction for the observed dependence, but it may not change the
estimand, confidence level, direction, or +10-bps hurdle.

## Economic rationale and expected failure mode

The rationale is behavioral and risk-transfer persistence, not a claim that an EMA predicts fair
value. BTC trades continuously, and information, leverage adjustment, and investor flows may be
absorbed over multiple hours or days. A fast average crossing above a slow average is a delayed
state-change indicator; remaining flat after a bearish cross may reduce participation in prolonged
declines.

The expected failure mode is whipsaw. Sideways or abruptly reversing markets can create repeated
late entries and exits whose gross movement is smaller than spread, slippage, and fees. EMA pairs
also encode highly correlated variants, making an isolated best pair especially likely to be a
selection artifact. The protocol therefore requires doubled-cost survival, neighborhood
stability, fold breadth, concentration limits, multiplicity control, and one untouched evaluation.

## Strategy contract held fixed

The study tests the implementation in `src/market/strategy/slow_trend.py` without adding filters or
changing its state machine:

- Product: BTC-USD spot; long or flat only; no shorting, leverage, derivatives, or pyramiding.
- Frequency: one-hour, UTC, close-confirmed candles.
- Indicator: Decimal dual EMA, each seeded by the simple mean of its first `period` closes.
- Entry: while flat, buy after `fast[t-1] <= slow[t-1]` and `fast[t] > slow[t]`.
- Exit: while long, sell the full position after `fast[t-1] >= slow[t-1]` and
  `fast[t] < slow[t]`.
- Decision time: after bar `t` is closed; baseline fill eligibility is bar `t+1` open.
- End of an admitted segment or evaluation window: expire an ineligible pending order and close
  remaining inventory through the engine's visible, fully costed terminal liquidation fill.
- Gap policy: never bridge a G1 gap. Each admitted segment revalidates and fully rewarms the EMAs.

No stop loss, take profit, volatility target, time-of-day filter, volume filter, regime switch,
cooldown change, alternative moving-average definition, or discretionary trade removal is allowed.
Such a change is a different hypothesis.

## Allowed information

### Strategy-visible inputs

Only these values may influence an entry or exit decision:

- ordered close prices from admitted, close-confirmed BTC-USD one-hour candles;
- the current long/flat position state; and
- the preregistered fast and slow EMA periods.

Timestamps may establish order and bar continuity but may not act as calendar or session features.

### Evaluator-only inputs

The research engine may use candle timestamps and OHLCV/data-quality fields to validate the G1
contract; next-bar open to execute; final close to liquidate; declared cost inputs; accounting and
portfolio state; and predeclared fold, holdout, stress, regime, and benchmark labels. These values
may measure a result but may not alter a strategy signal.

### Prohibited inputs

The study may not use future candles, the forming candle, alternate venues, other assets, order
book data, funding or derivatives data, blockchain data, news, sentiment, volume-derived signals,
G1 regime labels as a trading filter, or any manually chosen exclusion. It may not silently fill a
missing candle. Test-fold and holdout results may not influence parameter choice.

## Complete parameter family

Only `fast_ema` and `slow_ema` may vary:

```text
fast_ema candidates = {6, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 72}
slow_ema candidates = {18, 26, 36, 48, 72, 96, 120, 168, 240, 336}
admit pair only when 1.5 <= slow_ema / fast_ema <= 8
```

This produces exactly **36 preregistered pairs**, including the legacy `12/26` default. No
continuous optimizer, interpolated period, second-pass narrowed grid, or unregistered pair is
allowed. The 36 pairs form one multiple-testing family.

An immediate parameter neighbor changes exactly one coordinate by one position in its ordered
candidate list and still satisfies the ratio constraint. A promotable pair must have at least
three valid immediate neighbors; at least two thirds of those neighbors and the neighborhood
median must have positive stitched out-of-sample expectancy under primary costs. The selected
pair's expectancy may not exceed three times the positive neighborhood median. These rules reject
edge-of-grid and isolated-peak winners.

## Walk-forward selection and multiplicity rules

G3.2 must freeze chronological train, validation, test, and final-holdout boundaries before any
pair runs. G3.3 must register every one of the 36 pairs in every applicable fold, including errors,
zero-trade variants, and failures. Omitting a failed variant invalidates the family.

Within each development fold, parameter choice may use training and validation data only. Eligible
pairs are ranked by validation mean net trade return under primary costs. Ties are resolved, in
order, by lower maximum drawdown, lower fast period, and lower slow period. The chosen pair is then
run once on that fold's test interval. Test output is appended to the stitched out-of-sample record
and cannot trigger reselection for the same fold.

Candidate-screen inference across the 36-pair family must use one-sided tests with Holm-Bonferroni
family-wise error control at `alpha = 0.05`. Both raw and adjusted values must be retained. The final
holdout is one confirmatory evaluation of the already frozen selection rule and is not added to the
search family.

## Binding graduation and rejection criteria

The EMA hypothesis is rejected for strategy graduation if **any** of the following is false on the
stitched walk-forward out-of-sample evidence:

1. Aggregate net P&L and arithmetic mean net trade return are positive under primary costs.
2. The one-sided 95% block-bootstrap lower bound for mean net trade return exceeds **+10 bps**.
3. Aggregate net P&L and mean net trade return remain positive under doubled costs.
4. At least 70% of test folds have positive primary-cost net P&L.
5. At least 100 out-of-sample closed trades exist. This protocol preauthorizes no lower-frequency
   waiver.
6. No single trade and no single fold contributes more than 50% of stitched primary-cost net
   profit.
7. Primary-cost net P&L beats cash, and net P&L divided by maximum dollar NLV drawdown is strictly
   better than the G2 matched-notional buy-and-hold result. DCA is reported but is secondary.
8. Maximum account NLV drawdown is no more than 10% of starting cash; the 95% expected shortfall of
   closed-trade returns is no worse than -10% of entry notional; and the worst closed trade is no
   worse than -20% of entry notional.
9. The selected pair passes the preregistered immediate-neighborhood rules.
10. Results remain qualifying under the one-additional-bar execution delay and are not dependent
    on one contiguous G1 segment or one predeclared market regime.
11. Multiplicity-adjusted candidate evidence passes at family-wise `alpha = 0.05`.

Positive in-sample, training, validation, frictionless, annualized Sharpe, or one-parameter results
cannot substitute for a failed criterion.

## Untouched holdout decision

G3.8 may evaluate the final holdout exactly once after the complete pipeline, selected-pair rule,
bootstrap method, regime definitions, and stress implementation are frozen. The holdout passes
only if all of these are true:

- primary-cost net P&L is positive and mean net trade return exceeds +10 bps;
- the one-sided 95% block-bootstrap lower bound for mean net trade return exceeds zero;
- primary-cost net P&L beats cash and the matched-notional buy-and-hold risk objective;
- doubled-cost net P&L and mean net trade return are positive;
- drawdown, expected-shortfall, worst-trade, and 50% trade-concentration limits pass; and
- the selected pair is unchanged from the frozen pre-holdout pipeline.

The 100-trade and 70%-of-folds requirements apply to the combined stitched walk-forward evidence,
not to the single holdout interval. A holdout failure rejects this protocol version. The holdout
may never be moved, relabeled as training data, or used to tune a successor and still be called
untouched.
